# Code of Conduct

Nitely's project spaces should stay useful for technical work.

Expected behavior:

- Be direct, respectful, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Keep issue and PR discussions on the concrete behavior, risk, or tradeoff.
- Avoid sharing private credentials, customer data, or non-public repository
  contents in public channels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, personal attacks, deliberate
disruption, or publishing someone else's private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments and may restrict
participation when needed to keep the project workable and safe.
